Do I have to follow the plan exactly?

No. Every evening can be swapped, skipped or moved, and the shopping list updates to match.

Can it work around allergies?

Yes. Mark what to avoid once, and those ingredients stay out of every suggestion.

What if I only cook twice a week?

Pick just those evenings. Pillow Kitchen only plans the nights you choose.

Make this week feel softer.

Tell Pillow Kitchen what is in your fridge and which evenings are free. Your first plan takes a few minutes.
