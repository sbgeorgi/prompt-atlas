The studio

A garden is a long conversation, not a delivery.

Hollow & Fern is three gardeners and a drafting table. We design for the soil you actually have, the light you actually get, and the time you actually want to spend. Most of what we do is small, and all of it is tended.

Site reading

We walk the plot with a notebook: sun, drainage, wind, and where you really sit with a coffee.

Planting plans

Drawn by hand, labelled in plain language, with a bloom calendar that runs from February to November.

Seasonal keeping

Pruning, dividing, mulching and the small jobs that quietly decide whether a border thrives.

Honest advice

If a plant will sulk in your corner, we will say so, and suggest one that will not.

How a garden gets made

Four steps, no rush.

  1. 01

    Walk and listen

    One visit, two hours, tea. We measure, photograph and ask what you want to stop worrying about.

  2. 02

    Draw the plan

    A scaled plan, a planting list and a sensible order of works, so you can build in stages if you like.

  3. 03

    Plant in season

    Hard landscaping first, planting when the soil is ready, never to a calendar date that suits us.

  4. 04

    Come back

    A keeping visit each season for the first year, then as often as the garden asks.

What stays with you

Questions

Before you write to us

Do you work in very small spaces?

Yes, and we like them. Courtyards, balconies and front steps often get the most thought per square metre.

Can I build the plan in stages?

That is how most people do it. The plan arrives with an order of works so each stage stands up on its own.

What if I only want advice?

A seasonal visit is exactly that: a morning of walking the garden together and a written list of what to do next.

How far do you travel?

We keep to a day's round trip from the studio, so the same gardener can come back each season.
