README.TXT

Read this first

Most team notes end up scattered across chat threads, shared drives and half-finished wikis. Deskpad keeps them in one flat folder of plain text files that anyone can open, even without Deskpad.

Every page is a window. Open as many as you like, tile them, stack them, or pin the three that matter this week. Links between pages are written as double brackets and update themselves when you rename something.

There are no databases to run and no accounts to forget. Your notes sit on your disk and sync through a folder you already trust.

Small teams use it for meeting notes, runbooks, project logs and the odd recipe for the office kettle descaler.

    Download the installer for your system and run it.

FAQ.HLP
Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything, including search, runs on your machine. Sync happens when a connection returns.

Can I import my old wiki?

Drop a folder of Markdown or text files into your notes folder and they show up as pages.

What happens if I stop paying?

Nothing breaks. Your files stay yours and the app you installed keeps working.
