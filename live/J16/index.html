Fine
print

What file formats do you take?

PDF, AI, PSD and PNG at 300 dpi. A phone photo of a sketch is fine to start. We will help you separate colours.

Can I get a sample first?

Yes. For the Street run and above we print a physical swatch on your chosen stock for $25, credited back to your order.

How fast can you rush a job?

Most Mini runs ship in 72 hours for a 30 percent rush fee. Call us before 10 am Pacific and we will check the press calendar.

Do you print outside the US?

We ship worldwide by tracked courier. Duties are billed by the carrier, never hidden in our price.
