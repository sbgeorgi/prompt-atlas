What you learn

Four rules, no ornament.

Set by hand

Pick every sort from the case. You learn spacing because your fingers have to do it.

Flat colour

Red, blue, yellow, black. Overprint two inks and read what the paper does with them.

Grid first

Every layout starts as a ruled sheet. Margins are decided before words arrive.

Edit hard

One headline, one image, one action. Whatever is left over gets cut.

The shop

A press that still knows how to say no.

Our 1952 Vandercook proofing press prints one sheet at a time, which makes every decision visible. You cannot hide behind undo.

Tutors are working designers and printers, never more than six students per press.

How the six weeks run

  1. 01

    Look

    Weeks 1-2. Measure old posters, redraw their grids, set your first line of type.

  2. 02

    Make

    Weeks 3-5. Build your poster in three plates. Proof, mis-register, fix, proof again.

  3. 03

    Show

    Week 6. Edition of twenty, a wall to hang it on and a critique that is kind but exact.

Before you ask

Questions.

Do I need design experience?

No. Half of each cohort has never set type. We start from the case and the ruler.

What do I take home?

Your edition of prints, your plates, and a binder of grid studies from weeks one and two.

Is the shop accessible?

Ground floor, step-free entry, adjustable-height benches. Tell us what you need and we will set it up.

Can I defer a seat?

Yes, once, up to two weeks before the cohort starts, at no charge.
