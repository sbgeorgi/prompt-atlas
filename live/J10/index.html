01Specs

Built for the dead zone

Four things we refused to compromise on while designing the radio for search crews, trail builders and film units.

A.01Range

Self-healing routes

If a unit drops out, traffic finds another path through the mesh within seconds. Nobody has to reconfigure anything.

A.02Privacy

Encrypted by default

Every message is sealed with a key you load on your own kit. Nullband has no servers that could read it.

A.03Power

Three-day battery

Low-duty radio and an e-paper screen keep a unit alive through a long weekend. Charge over USB-C or a solar panel.

A.04Build

Sealed and glove-ready

Big physical keys, a rubber-sealed case and a clip that holds on a pack strap through a scramble.

02Method

Online in four steps

No account, no app store, no coverage map. Everything happens on the radio itself.

  1. 01

    Unbox and charge

    Each kit ships at half charge. Top it up over USB-C and the unit is ready to pair.

  2. 02

    Load your key

    Hold two units together to copy the crew key. It never leaves the hardware.

  3. 03

    Spread out

    Walk, drive or climb. Units announce themselves and form routes as you move.

  4. 04

    Talk or type

    Push to talk for voice, or send a short text with the keypad. Delivery receipts show on the screen.

05Questions

Before you buy

Is a licence needed to operate it?

Nullband runs on licence-free ISM bands in most regions. Check the rules for your country before first use; our regional sheet lists limits per market.

How many hops will a message take?

Up to seven hops between any two units. Each extra hop adds a small delay, which you will see on the delivery receipt.

Can I use my phone with it?

The radio works on its own. A Bluetooth link to a phone is optional and only used for typing longer messages.
