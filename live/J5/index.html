IAtelier

A house should settle into its place, and then ask very little of the people who live in it.

We begin with the ground, the orientation and the oldest wall on the site. Nothing is added until we understand what is already doing its work, and what has been quietly asking to be removed.

Materials are chosen for how they age: lime plaster, oiled oak, honed limestone, unlacquered brass. Each is sourced within a day's drive, and every one is selected by hand with the household present.

VIQuestions

Asked often

If yours is not here, write to the studio and a person will answer.

How many projects do you take each year?

Four to six. The studio is intentionally small so that a principal is present at every site visit.

Do you work outside Portugal?

Yes, within southern Europe. We travel for the initial visit and then from the Lisbon studio, with fortnightly calls and monthly site days.

Can we keep part of the existing house?

Usually we will ask you to. Old walls, stairs and floors tend to hold the character a new build spends decades trying to earn.

How long does a residence take?

Design runs six to nine months. Construction depends on the site, typically twelve to eighteen months.

Let us walk your threshold.

Write to the studio with a few lines about the place and the people. We reply within two working days, always in person.
