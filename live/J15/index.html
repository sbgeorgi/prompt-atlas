The Studio

A fixture should know its room

Most lighting is chosen from a page and fitted to a ceiling that was never measured for it. We work the other way round. We begin with the height of the hall, the run of the stair and the colour of the stone, and only then draw the fixture.

Every piece is cut, spun and assembled in one workshop. Frames are solid brass, shades are hand-finished glass, and every joint is meant to be seen. We keep the ornament geometric and leave the light to do the rest.

The Craft

Built from six honest parts

Solid Brass

Turned and welded frames, finished in satin, aged or polished, never plated over a lesser metal.

Fluted Glass

Opal and clear shades with vertical flutes that soften the lamp without hiding the frame.

Stepped Forms

Tiered silhouettes sized to the ceiling height, so the fixture reads from the street and from the landing.

Warm Dimming

2200 to 2700 K lamps that shift warmer as they dim, wired to the control system you already have.

Measured Mounts

Canopies and chains are drawn from a site survey so the weight lands on structure, not plaster.

Service Access

Every shade lifts off without tools, and every lamp is reachable from a standard ladder.

The Process

Questions

Before you write to us

Can you match an existing fixture?

Yes. Send photographs and any measurements you have, and we will draw the original and the match side by side before anything is cut.

Which finishes are available?

Satin brass, aged brass and polished brass. Glass comes in opal, clear and smoked, each with the same flute pattern.

Do you work outside the city?

We travel for surveys and installations within a day's drive. Further away, we ship crated pieces with full hanging drawings.

What if the ceiling cannot carry the weight?

Our mount plan states the load and the fixing points. If the structure needs help, we say so before the quote, not after.
