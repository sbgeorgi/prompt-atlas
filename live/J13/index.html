$faq

      
Where are my notes stored?
In the folder you choose, as ordinary Markdown files. Notch never moves them somewhere hidden.

      
Can I use Notch without an account?
Yes. The Local plan needs no account and no network connection.

      
What happens if I stop paying?
Your files stay exactly where they are. You lose sync and version history, nothing else.

      
Does it work with other editors?
Any editor that opens text files works alongside Notch. Changes show up as soon as they are saved.
