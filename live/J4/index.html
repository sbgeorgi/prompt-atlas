We keep the paper the world throws away.

Independent archive of printed ephemera, est. 1998, Rotterdam.

Open Tuesday to Saturday, by appointment.

Handbills, ticket stubs, shop labels and municipal forms: the quiet printing of everyday life, cleaned, described and shelved so anyone can ask to see it.

Method

Four steps, in this order, for every item.

  1. 1

    Receive

    Boxes arrive from estates, shops and demolitions. Each is photographed before it is opened.

  2. 2

    Stabilise

    Dry-brush cleaning, flattening under weight, and removal of rusted fasteners. Nothing is repaired unless it will fall apart.

  3. 3

    Describe

    A volunteer writes one plain sentence about what the item is and who made it, then a second person checks it.

  4. 4

    Shelve

    Items are filed by series and shown in the reading room within a week of their catalogue entry going live.

Questions

And what they ask before they sign up.

Do I need to be a researcher?

No. Most readers are curious neighbours, designers and family historians. Staff will help you find a starting shelf.

Can I photograph items myself?

Yes, without flash, on the reading room's copy stand. Reader and Patron cards include monthly scans made by our staff.

Do you accept donations of paper?

We do, after a short conversation. Write to us with a description or a photograph before sending a box.

Is the reading room accessible?

The room is on the ground floor with level entry. Tables adjust in height and large-print catalogue cards are available on request.

Bring us something small.
