The product

A quieter way to finish the month

Most teams close the books by chasing differences between a bank statement and a spreadsheet. Harbor Ledger keeps those two sides in conversation all month, flagging what does not line up while the context is still fresh.

Reviewers see one queue of open items, each with the source transaction, a suggested match and a plain note of why it is unresolved. Nothing is posted until someone approves it.

Questions, answered plainly

Can Harbor Ledger move money?

No. Bank connections are read-only. The product reads transactions and balances; it cannot initiate payments or transfers.

What happens to entries I do not approve?

They stay in the open-items queue with their suggested match. Nothing is posted to the ledger until a reviewer approves it.

Can my accountant get access?

Yes. Invite them with a reviewer or read-only role, scoped to the workspaces you choose.

How do I get my data out?

Every report and the full entry history export as CSV, and the same data is available through the API.
