Practice

Four areas, one standard of care

We take on fewer matters than we are asked to, so that each receives a partner's full attention.

  1. Estates & trusts

    Wills, trusts and the quiet administration that follows a loss.

  2. Property

  3. Succession

  4. Dispute resolution

    Mediation first; the courtroom only when it serves the client.

Method

From first letter to final signature

A matter moves in four steps. You will always know which one we are in.

  1. 01

    Listen

    A first meeting without a clock. We ask what you want to protect, and for whom, before we propose a structure.

  2. 02

    Set out the options

    A written memorandum of two or three routes, with their costs, risks and tax consequences stated side by side.

  3. 03

    Draft

    Instruments are drafted in plain language, then reviewed with you line by line, in person if you prefer.

  4. 04

    Keep

    Signed documents are held in our vault and reviewed annually, at no charge, for as long as you remain a client.

Questions

Asked often, answered once

How soon can we begin?

Most first meetings take place within two weeks. If a matter is urgent, a partner will speak with you the same day.

Do you work with clients overseas?

Yes. We coordinate with counsel in eleven jurisdictions and hold meetings by secure video when travel is impractical.

What happens if our circumstances change?

Tell us. Reviews are part of every engagement, and amending an instrument we drafted carries no additional fee in the first five years.

Who will I actually speak with?

A named partner, for the life of the matter. You will have their direct line and their address.
