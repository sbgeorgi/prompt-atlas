Questions

Good things to know before you start.

Do I need to replace any of my current tools?

No. Conduit sits between them. Your forms, sheets and inboxes stay exactly where they are.

Who can build a workflow?

Anyone on the team. Rules are written in plain language, and technical users can add custom steps when they want to.

What happens if a step fails?

The workflow pauses, keeps the data, and notifies the owner with the exact step and reason so it can be retried.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Records and activity history can be exported at any time on every plan.
