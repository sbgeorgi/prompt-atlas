We move the city faster than traffic

Northbound is a bicycle courier cooperative. Riders collect and deliver parcels, documents and fresh produce across the old town and the harbour districts, on routes we have ridden for years.

Same-hour pickup on weekdays. Every delivery is signed for, photographed and confirmed to your phone before the rider rolls on.

03 - How it worksFour moves

1

Book

Tell us where the parcel is and where it is going. Pick up now or choose a time.

2

Match

We assign the nearest rider and send you their name and photo.

3

Ride

Follow the rider on the map. They take the quickest route, not the longest.

4

Sign

The receiver signs, we photograph the handover and confirm it to you.

No engine. No excuse.

Quiet streets

Bicycles do not idle in traffic and they do not need parking, so the last hundred metres are the easiest part of the trip.

Fair pay

Every rider is a member of the cooperative and shares in what we earn, which is why they care about your parcel.

Clean ledger

One monthly statement with every run itemised, so your accounts team never has to chase a receipt.

06 - QuestionsAsked often

How fast is a rush run?

Most rush runs are collected within the hour on weekdays. The ride itself depends on distance, and the rider will message you when they set off.

What can a courier carry?

Anything that fits in a pannier or a cargo crate: documents, boxes, produce, flowers. If you are unsure, send us a photo and we will tell you straight away.

Do you ride in the rain?

Yes. Parcels travel in waterproof liners, and riders swap routes to avoid flooded underpasses.

How do I pause my weekly round?

Message us before ten the day before and the round is skipped with no charge. Resume whenever you like.
